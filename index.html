Aerospace assurance crosswalk

A traceable map from proposed aviation card fields to public source topics. It helps reviewers ask better questions; it does not approve a system or maintenance release.

Source status checked 2026-10-12

What this map cannot prove

These rows relate disclosure fields to source topics. They do not show conformity, certification, airworthiness approval, maintenance release, or regulatory filing.

EASA material here is proposed, the FAA roadmap is guidance for future methods, ED-324 is draft, NIST AI RMF is voluntary, and licensed aviation standards are referenced by identifier only. Every row states its scope and limit.

Field-to-source map

These are review prompts. Follow the primary source and the applicable certification or maintenance process before making a real-world decision.

  1. EASA · proposal

    Concept Of Operations

    Card fields: system.intended_use, human_ai_teaming.role

    Reference: DS.AI.100 at EASA

    Applies when: A vendor describing an AI-based aviation system's concept of operations and end-user role.

    Why linked: The fields disclose intended use and the allocation of work between system and human.

    Limit: A filled card does not demonstrate the proposal's objective or an accepted ConOps.

  2. EASA · proposal

    Ai Level Classification

    Card fields: assurance.easa_ai_level, human_ai_teaming.role, human_ai_teaming.human_override_available

    Reference: DS.AI.110 / AMC1 AI.110 at EASA

    Applies when: Vendor-declared proposed EASA AI level for the relevant high-level task.

    Why linked: Records the claimed level and the human authority posture for review.

    Limit: Level 3B is reserved in the proposal. Level 2A, 2B and 3A have different human authority; a boolean alone cannot establish correct classification.

  3. EASA · proposal

    Operational Domain

    Card fields: odd.operating_conditions, odd.excluded_conditions, odd.condition_evidence, odd.coverage_evidence_refs

    Reference: DS.AI.120 / AMC1 AI.120 at EASA

    Applies when: An AI-based system with defined operating conditions.

    Why linked: Declares operating limits and links each declared condition to evidence references.

    Limit: Linked evidence is not verified by this crosswalk; declared-bin coverage is not real-world representativeness or approval.

  4. EASA · proposal

    Risk Assurance Allocation

    Card fields: assurance.target_dal, assurance.standards_posture

    Reference: DS.AI.130 at EASA

    Applies when: When an applicant has a documented safety assessment and allocated assurance level.

    Why linked: Captures a vendor's declared target and related standards posture for later review.

    Limit: No DAL can be inferred from the AI level alone. The card cannot assign, verify, or approve a DAL.

  5. EASA · guidance

    Learning Assurance

    Card fields: learning_assurance.process_summary, learning_assurance.evidence_refs, data_management.dataset_manifest_refs

    Reference: Issue 2, learning assurance topic at EASA

    Applies when: Learning-based aviation systems within the concept paper's Level 1 or 2 scope.

    Why linked: Points a reviewer to process and dataset evidence without copying restricted datasets into the card.

    Limit: Concept-paper alignment is not an EASA approval or complete learning-assurance demonstration.

  6. FAA · roadmap

    Faa Learning System

    Card fields: system.intended_use, learning_assurance.process_summary, data_management.split_policy

    Reference: Guiding principles: learned versus learning AI; work within the aviation ecosystem at FAA

    Applies when: FAA-oriented safety-assurance discussion for AI in aircraft and related systems.

    Why linked: Prompts explicit intended-use and learning-process documentation.

    Limit: The roadmap sets research and policy direction, not a product-specific certification method.

  7. EUROCAE · draft

    Ed324 Status

    Card fields: assurance.standards_posture

    Reference: ED-324 (draft); ARP6983 (joint work identifier) at EUROCAE

    Applies when: Only where the eventual ML process standard is relevant to the system and applicant.

    Why linked: A status field can disclose that the standard is being monitored or evaluated.

    Limit: No published ED-324 objective or conformity claim is made. Target publication dates can move.

  8. SAE International · licensed published

    Arp4754B Applicability

    Card fields: assurance.target_dal, assurance.standards_posture

    Reference: ARP4754B; objective ID not verified at SAE International

    Applies when: Aircraft and system development where the applicant identifies an applicable certification basis; not automatically MRO analytics.

    Why linked: Records an identifier-level standards posture and allocated system-level assurance target.

    Limit: Licensed objective text and objective numbers are not reproduced or asserted.

  9. RTCA · licensed published

    Do178C Applicability

    Card fields: assurance.standards_posture

    Reference: DO-178C / ED-12C; objective ID not verified at RTCA

    Applies when: Airborne software when it forms part of the applicant's accepted assurance approach; not automatically off-aircraft maintenance tools.

    Why linked: Records that a vendor cites the standard identifier and can link its own evidence.

    Limit: This row does not claim a particular DO-178C objective, software level, or compliance.

  10. NIST · voluntary

    Nist Context

    Card fields: system.intended_use, odd.operating_conditions

    Reference: MAP 1.1 at NIST

    Applies when: Voluntary risk-management context for organizations using or developing AI.

    Why linked: Makes purpose and operating context explicit for human review.

    Limit: NIST AI RMF is voluntary and not an aviation approval checklist.

  11. NIST · voluntary

    Nist Measurement

    Card fields: odd.condition_evidence, data_management.leakage_check_ref

    Reference: MEASURE 1.1 at NIST

    Applies when: Voluntary choice of measurement methods for significant identified AI risks.

    Why linked: Points to bounded ODD evidence and leakage checks to be judged on their merits.

    Limit: A reference or hash does not prove measurement quality, dataset rights, or representativeness.

  12. EASA · regulation

    Eu Mro Release Separation

    Card fields: human_review.staff_id, human_review.authority_ref, signature.signature_b64url

    Reference: 145.A.50 at EASA

    Applies when: EASA Part-145 maintenance where an appropriately authorised certifying staff member issues a separate release to service.

    Why linked: A review card can point to a human and the organisation's separate authorisation record.

    Limit: A review-card signature does not verify staff privileges or issue a certificate of release to service.

  13. U.S. Government Publishing Office / FAA · regulation

    Us Mro Record Separation

    Card fields: finding.summary, human_review.staff_id, human_review.authority_ref

    Reference: 14 CFR § 43.9(a) at U.S. Government Publishing Office / FAA

    Applies when: U.S. maintenance records under this section; other inspection or operator programmes may have different rules.

    Why linked: An AI review card can travel as supporting evidence alongside the controlled maintenance record.

    Limit: It does not replace the required work description, completion date, or qualifying signer's record.
